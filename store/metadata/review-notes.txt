Notes for App Review:

No account or sign-in is required — the app is fully usable immediately after
onboarding (enter a name and add a dog).

BACKGROUND LOCATION: The app declares the "location" background mode. It is
used solely so that an actively recording dog walk keeps tracking when the
screen locks or the phone goes in a pocket. The app requests only
"When In Use" authorisation; background updates run exclusively during a walk
the user has explicitly started, and stop when the walk is finished or
discarded. Location is stored on-device only and never transmitted.

To test walk recording: complete onboarding, tap "Start Walk" on the Today
tab, grant location when asked, and use the simulator/device location. Finish
the walk to see the summary and statistics.

The Explore tab uses MapKit local search to list dog-friendly places near the
user's current location, sorted by distance. Results are labelled as coming
from Apple Maps, with a caution that dog-access rules are unverified.

All data is stored locally; the app has no server, no analytics SDK and no
third-party dependencies.
