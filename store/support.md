Companion is a dog-walking tracker for iPhone.

## Common questions

**Why does Companion ask for my location?**
To record your walk route and, in Explore, to sort nearby dog-friendly places
by distance. Location is only read while you use those features and never
leaves your phone. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

**My walk stopped recording when my screen locked.**
Make sure Companion has location access set to "While Using the App" in
Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. An active walk keeps
recording with the screen locked.

**How do I delete my data?**
Profile → delete options let you remove individual walks, your history, or
everything. Deleting the app removes all data too — nothing is stored outside
your phone.
