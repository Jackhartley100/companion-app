Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 July 2026

Companion is a dog-walking tracker made by Jack Hartley. This policy describes what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your iPhone.

What Companion stores

  • Your name, your dogs' profiles and photos
  • Walks you record: route, distance, time and any notes or photos you add
  • Goals, achievements and places you save

All of this is stored only on your device. Companion has no user accounts, no server, and no cloud sync. We cannot see your data, because it is never sent to us.

Location

Companion uses your location for two things, both on your device:

  • Recording a walk — to draw your route on the map and measure distance. Recording continues while your phone is locked, only during an active walk.
  • Explore — to sort nearby dog-friendly places by distance. This is a one-off reading each time you open the tab, not tracking.

Your location and routes are never transmitted off the device. Nearby-place results are provided by Apple Maps; those lookups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What Companion does not do

  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking, profiling or data sales
  • No third-party data sharing of any kind

Deleting your data

Everything can be deleted in the app: individual walks, your activity history, or all data at once (Profile → delete options). Deleting the app also removes all data, since nothing exists anywhere else.

Photos

When you add a photo of your dog or a walk, you pick it with Apple's photo picker. Companion only receives the photo you choose and stores it on-device.

Children

Companion does not knowingly collect any personal information — from anyone — because it collects nothing at all.

Changes

If a future version of Companion changes any of the above (for example, adding optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly described in the app.
